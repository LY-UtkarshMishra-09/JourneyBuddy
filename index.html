💌 Our Story

Handcrafted for Wanderer Souls ✨

JourneyBuddy was born from the pure joy of keeping physical paper travel scrapbooks: pasting train stubs, collecting pressed flowers, doodling coffee cups, and sticking polaroids with washi tape.

We wanted to bring that exact soft, cozy feeling to the digital world. No complicated spreadsheets or corporate business calendars—just pastel colors, cute checkboxes, and heartwarming memories!

📌

Our Core Promise

100% aesthetic, offline-friendly, and always fun to look at!

📌

Packing Rule
